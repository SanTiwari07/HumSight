          Limitations

          • Indicative, camera-based measurement. Not a certified ISO assessment.
          • Frequency is valid only up to half the capture frame rate; record at 2.5× the fault frequency or more.
          • Amplitude is in image pixels unless an image scale was given.
          • Compression, low texture and lighting flicker can mask or fake sub-pixel motion.
